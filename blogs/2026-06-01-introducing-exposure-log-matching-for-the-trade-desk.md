Hightouch and The Trade Desk have partnered to launch Exposure Log Matching, enabling brands to resolve ad exposure data directly to their own customer IDs inside the data warehouse. The solution allows analysts and AI agents to quickly resolve exposure logs from The Trade Desk to consented customer and household IDs without vendor lock-in, delivering faster measurement insights than traditional third-party identity approaches.
